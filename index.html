    The details.
    
      
    
        
    How do I pay?
    Membership is paid monthly. We’ll confirm payment options with you when you join.
    
        
    How do I book my washes?
    Message us on WhatsApp whenever you want a wash, or set a regular day and time with us. Members get first pick of slots.

    We keep a record of every wash on your plan. Ask us any time and we’ll tell you how many you’ve used and how many are left this month.

    Just message us before your next month starts and we’ll switch or stop your plan.

    Privacy
    
      
    Your privacy
    
      
      
    What we collect, why, and how to ask us to change or delete it.
    
    
    
  
    
  
    
    
    
      
    Fresh Beast Detailers is a mobile car detailing business serving Kingston, St. Andrew and St. Catherine. This notice explains how we handle the information you give us through this website. Last updated: November 2026
    
      
    
        
    What we collect
      
          
    • Contact details — your name, phone number and email address.
      • 
          
    • Booking details — your vehicle, the package and extras you choose, your preferred date and time, and the address where we’ll do the work.
      • 
          
    • Account history — washes we’ve done for you, Beast Tokens earned and used, and rewash or booking requests.
      • 
          
    • Wash photos — a photo we take of your vehicle after each wash, to confirm the work was done.
      • 
        
    
        
    How we use it
      
          
    • To book, confirm and carry out your detail.
      • 
          
    • To contact you about your bookings, rewashes and membership.
      • 
          
    • To keep track of your washes and Beast Token balance.
      • 
          
    • We don’t sell your information or use it for advertising.
      • 
        
    
        
    Where it’s kept
    Account information is stored in a secure online database (Supabase). Only you and the Fresh Beast team can see your account. Wash photos are private — only you and our team can view them.
    When you send a booking through WhatsApp, that message is also handled under WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.
    
        
    Cookies & storage
    We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores a small amount of information only to keep you signed in and to remember that you’ve already seen the opening animation.
    
        
    How long we keep it
    We keep your information while your account is open, so your wash history and tokens stay accurate. If you ask us to delete your account, we’ll remove your information unless we need to keep a record of a payment.
    
        
    Your choices
    You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Email detailers.fb@gmail.com or WhatsApp (876) 525-2174.
